Privacy Policy

Dream Lip Sync (“Dream Lip Sync & Face Dance AI” on Google Play and “Lip Sync Face” on the App Store)
Effective date: 9 October 2026
Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are. The Dream Lip Sync mobile application (“the App”) is developed and operated by YeahAI Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”), which is also the data controller responsible for your personal data. Contact: y@yeahapps.me.

What this policy covers. This policy explains what personal data the App collects on both the Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store) versions, why we collect it, how it is used, who processes it, how long we keep it and the choices and rights you have.

1. The short version

  • The App turns a photo into a talking or singing video. To do that, the photo you choose, the voice or music you record, or the text you type are sent to our servers and to AI processing providers that generate the video for us. The App cannot create a video without this.
  • We use your photos, recordings and text only to create the video you asked for. We do not sell them, do not use them for advertising, do not use them to identify people and do not create biometric or face-recognition templates.
  • Your finished videos are saved on your device. You decide whether to save them to your gallery or share them.
  • The App has no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • All data sent to and from our servers and providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to y@yeahapps.me.

3. Device permissions we request

  • Camera — only if you choose to take a new photo in the App.
  • Photos / storage — only to let you pick a photo and to save finished videos to your gallery when you choose to.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record your voice or music for a video.
  • Internet — to send your request for processing and receive the result.

You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings. Without camera or photo access you cannot add a photo; without microphone access you can still use typed text with an AI voice.

4. How we use your data

  • Create the talking or singing video you request, including AI voices and avatar styles.
  • Process purchases and restore subscriptions.
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your photos, recordings or text to train AI models, and we do not use your data to serve advertising or build advertising profiles.

5. Who processes your data

Service providers / sub-processors that process data on our behalf under contract and only for the purposes described above:

  • Our video-generation server (dreamfaceapp.com) — receives your photo and recordings to create the animated video;
  • LightX (api.lightxeditor.com) — receives your photo only when you use AI avatar styles;
  • ElevenLabs — receives the text you type to generate the AI voice;
  • Google Firebase (Android) — analytics and crash reporting;
  • Apphud (iOS) — subscription status;
  • Google Play and Apple App Store — payments and subscriptions; we receive only purchase/entitlement status.

These providers may only use your data to provide their service to us. We may also disclose data to authorities or courts where required by law, or to a successor entity in a merger or acquisition that must honour this policy.

We do not sell personal data and do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising as defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

6. Data retention

  • Photos, recordings and text you submit — kept on processing servers only as long as needed to generate and deliver your video, and deleted within 30 days at the latest.
  • Generated videos — stored on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • App user ID and request records — kept while you use the App and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Crash and analytics data — kept by Firebase according to its retention settings, generally no longer than 14 months.
  • Purchase records — kept as long as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete generated videos at any time in the App. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on your device. To ask us to delete data held on our servers, email y@yeahapps.me with the subject “Delete my data”. We will complete the request within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records by law (for example purchase records). Deleting data or the App does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions or in your Apple ID subscription settings.

8. Security

Data exchanged with our servers and providers is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We restrict access to personal data to the people and providers who need it. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a personal data breach where required by law.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights. To exercise any of them, email y@yeahapps.me. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30 days, or 45 days under the CCPA) and may need to verify your request.

  • GDPR / UK GDPR (EEA, UK, Switzerland): access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawal of consent at any time. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): right to know, delete and correct personal information, right to opt out of sale or sharing (we do neither), right to limit use of sensitive personal information, and right to non-discrimination.
  • Other regions (for example Brazil LGPD, Canada PIPEDA, Australia Privacy Act) may grant similar rights; we will honour lawful requests.

10. Photos of other people and responsible use

Only upload photos of yourself or of people who have given you permission, and do not use the App to impersonate, harass or mislead anyone. If you believe a video was created from your photo without permission, contact us and we will help remove any data we hold.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact y@yeahapps.me and we will delete it.

12. International data transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and, where applicable, the UK Addendum.

13. App-store disclosures

This policy is intended to match the Google Play Data safety section and the App Store privacy labels for the App. The App contains no advertising SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the dates at the top and, for material changes, notify you in the App or by other appropriate means. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
