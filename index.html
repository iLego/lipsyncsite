What you can do

Talking photo from text

Lip sync to your voice or a song

Tap the mic and talk, sing or play your favorite song or a trending sound. The photo moves its lips to the recording: a singing photo, a birthday song or a funny voice message.

How to make a photo talk

  1. Add a photo. Upload or take a clear, front-facing photo.
  2. Add sound. Type a script and pick an AI voice, or record your voice or music.
  3. Tap Create. The AI generates your talking photo video.
  4. Save and share. Keep it in your gallery or send it straight away.

Tips for the most natural lip sync

  • Use a well-lit photo with one face looking at the camera.
  • Keep the mouth visible; avoid sunglasses and heavy shadows.
  • Short, clear phrases give the most natural result.
