Frequently asked questions

How do I make a photo talk?
Upload or take a clear, front-facing photo, add sound — type a text and choose an AI voice, or record your voice or music — and tap Create.

The result doesn’t look natural.
Use a well-lit photo with one face looking at the camera, keep the mouth visible and avoid sunglasses or heavy shadows. Short, clear phrases give the best lip sync.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?
Sign in with the same Google account or Apple ID and open the subscription screen in the app.

How do I delete my data?
Delete videos in the app, and email us with the subject “Delete my data” to remove data from our servers. See the Privacy Policy.
